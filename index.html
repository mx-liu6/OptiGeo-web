OptiGeo: Efficient Monocular Geometry for Embodied Perception in Optically Challenging Scenes

An efficient monocular geometry framework for robust embodied perception under optically challenging visual conditions.

Abstract

We present OptiGeo, an efficient monocular geometry framework for embodied perception in optically challenging scenes. OptiGeo targets reliable single-image 3D understanding under visual conditions that commonly degrade embodied systems, including difficult illumination, reflections, glare, blur, and other optical disturbances. By emphasizing efficient geometry estimation and robustness in challenging optical environments, OptiGeo supports embodied agents that must perceive, navigate, and interact in real-world scenes where visual signals are imperfect. Extensive experiments demonstrate that OptiGeo delivers accurate and stable monocular geometry while maintaining practical efficiency for embodied perception.
